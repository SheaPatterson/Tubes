# Cabinet & Microphone Combinations

Sound isn't just about the amp—it's about the cabinet resonance and the mic character.

## 📦 Cabinets
- **Winston 4x12**: Closed-back, punchy, classic Celestion G12.
- **US Steel 4x12**: Oversized, massive low-end resonance, V30s.
- **Twanger 1x12**: Open-back, spacious, Alnico Blue simulation.
- **Fuzzy 2x12**: Mid-heavy, focused, vintage character.

## 🎙️ Microphones
- **Dynamic (SM57)**: Aggressive, mid-forward, the high-gain standard.
- **Ribbon (R121)**: Dark, warm, realistic "distance" feel.
- **Condenser (C414)**: Full-range, crisp, detailed.

## 📏 Positioning Presets
- **Cap**: Brightest, high attack.
- **Cap-Edge**: Balanced, traditional studio sound.
- **Cone**: Warm, flat, reduced high-end.
- **Room**: 3-meter distance simulation for "space" and perspective.
