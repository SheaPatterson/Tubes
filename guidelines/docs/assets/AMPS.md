# Amplifier Models: The Catalog of Power

Our simulation includes hand-picked legends, reimagined with sub-pixel precision and algorithmic accuracy.

## 🇬🇧 Winston CHL (Marshall DSL Emulation)
- **Voice**: Aggressive British mid-range.
- **Tube Profile**: 3x 12AX7, 2x EL34.
- **Channels**: Clean, Crunch, Overdrive (Lead 1/2).
- **Unique Feature**: "Deep" switch for resonance shifting on the low end.

## 🇺🇸 US Steel Plate (Mesa Rectifier Emulation)
- **Voice**: Massive, crushing high-gain with saggy rectifier dynamics.
- **Tube Profile**: 5x 12AX7, 2x 6L6.
- **Channels**: Clean, Raw, Vintage, Modern.
- **Unique Feature**: Simulation of the Diode vs. Tube rectifier switch.

## 🇺🇸 Twanger Banger (Fender Bassman Emulation)
- **Voice**: The foundational clean/crunch that started it all.
- **Tube Profile**: 3x 12AX7, 2x 5881.
- **Unique Feature**: Highly sensitive "Bright" input simulation.

## 🧪 Fizzle 0505 (Peavy 5150 Emulation)
- **Voice**: The brown sound evolved. High-gain fizz and clarity.
- **Tube Profile**: 5x 12AX7, 2x 6L6.
- **Unique Feature**: Extreme "Resonance" control response.
