# FX Pedal Repository: The Virtual Board

Each pedal is modeled from the ground up, including transistor clipping and op-amp slew rates.

## 🟢 TOKOYO Tube Screamer
- **Basis**: Ibanez TS808.
- **Function**: Mid-boost overdrive.
- **Circuit**: JRC4558D op-amp simulation.

## 🟠 MAC Distortion +
- **Basis**: MXR Distortion+.
- **Function**: Gritty, hard-clipping distortion.
- **Circuit**: Hard-diode clipping profile.

## 🔘 KING Chorus
- **Basis**: BOSS CE series.
- **Function**: Lush, analog-style modulation.
- **Circuit**: BBD (Bucket Brigade Device) simulation with accurate clock noise.

## ⬛ Manhattan Big Muff
- **Basis**: EHX Big Muff Pi.
- **Function**: Sustained, creamy fuzz.
- **Circuit**: Four-stage transistor gain cascade.
