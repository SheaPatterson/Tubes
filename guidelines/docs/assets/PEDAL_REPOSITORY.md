# Detail of FX Pedals: Circuit Translation

We utilize a novel approach of translating various transistors, op-amps, and physical circuits into database-quantifiable information.

## 🔳 The Pedal Repository
- **MAC (MXR)**: Precision compression and iconic phasing.
- **KING (BOSS)**: The industry standard for overdrives and digital delays.
- **Manhattan (EHX)**: High-character fuzzy circuits and modulation.
- **TOKOYO (Ibanez)**: The quintessential tube screaming Mid-boost.

## 📟 Digital Multi-Effects: The KING-90
We offer a fully digital recreation of the **KING ME-90**.
- **Interface**: Easy-to-adjust knobs, multiple gain stages, and integrated amp models.
- **Hidden Power**: A unique ability to combine several classic circuits into one optimized digital chain with zero noise floor.

## 🔬 Transistor Mapping
- **Silicon vs. Germanium**: Modeling the temperature sensitivity and clipping characteristics of different transistor types.
- **Op-Amp Slew Rate**: Simulating how fast an op-amp can respond to transients, affecting the "clarity" of distortion pedals.
