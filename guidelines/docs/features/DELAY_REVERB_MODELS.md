# Spatial Effects: Delay & Reverb Models

Our spatial engine uses high-fidelity convolution and algorithmic delay lines to create "depth" in the simulation.

## ⏳ Delay Models
- **Digital Delay (KING)**: Crystal clear, repeating every detail with 0-2000ms range.
- **Carbon Delay (MAC)**: Warm, bucket-brigade style with dark repeats and optional modulation.
- **Tape Echo**: Modeling the wow-and-flutter and saturation of classic reel-to-reel units.

## 🌊 Reverb Engines
- **Spring Reverb**: Specifically modeled for "Twanger Banger" and "Winston" vintage tones.
- **Plate Reverb**: Dense, metallic, and smooth studio-grade reverb.
- **Room / Hall Convolution**: Using IR data from world-class studio spaces to place the cabinet in a realistic 3D room.

## 🔳 Implementation
- **Ping-Pong Mode**: For stereo widening in the live dashboard.
- **Sync to Tempo**: Automatic alignment with internal or MIDI clock.
