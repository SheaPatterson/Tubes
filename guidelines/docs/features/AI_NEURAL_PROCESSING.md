# AI Neural Layer: Training & Inference

The "Next Gen" tier adds a layer of AI that transforms a good simulation into an indistinguishable one.

## 🧠 Neural Architecture
- **RNN/LSTM**: Used to analyze the temporal dynamics of pick attack and tube "sag" over time.
- **WaveNet-style Synthesis**: To enhance the high-frequency "fizz" and "air" using generative modeling trained on studio-quality recordings of the real amps.

## ☁️ Cloud Integration
- **Ollama on VPS**: Our dedicated server handles the heavy lifting of inferencing when the user opts for the Next Gen subscription.
- **Weights Sync**: Small-footprint neural weights are synchronized to the local host for faster real-time response, while the cloud handles the ultra-high fidelity refinement.

## 🎯 Accuracy Goals
- **Profile Matching**: Analyzing a specific player's tone from a 30-second audio clip and generating a "Signature" signal chain that matches it.
- **Real-time Enhancement**: Dynamically adjusting tube bias and sag values based on the "Intention" of the player (detected via AI analysis of picking dynamics).
