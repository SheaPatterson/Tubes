# Marketing Strategy: Dominating the Digital Realm

To compete with giants like Positive Grid and IK Multimedia, our marketing must be as bold as our product.

## 📣 Core Messaging
- **The AI Advantage**: "Don't just model circuits. Model reality."
- **Transparency**: Open-sourcing our "Classic" engine while keeping the "Neural Layer" as our crown jewel.
- **Community First**: Partnering with niche YouTube guitarists (Tone chasers) to demonstrate the AI Profiling feature.

## 📈 Channels
1. **Developer Open-Source**: Releasing a base WASM DSP library to gain trust in the scientific/coding community.
2. **Influencer Collaboration**: Sending "Signature Capture" kits to professional studio engineers.
3. **SEO**: High-ranking technical articles on "Stage-by-Stage Tube Modeling vs. Traditional Snapshot IRs."

## 🗓️ Launch Stages
- **Alpha**: Invite-only for 50 professional producers.
- **Beta**: Free tier open to the public; first 1000 users get a "Lifetime" discount on Classic subscription.
- **V1.0**: Big launch with "Next Gen" AI tiers.
