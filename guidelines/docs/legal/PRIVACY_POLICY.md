# Secured Privacy Policy

## 🛡️ Data Guarding
- **Audio Privacy**: Your raw input signal is NEVER stored centrally unless you explicitly use the "Signature Capture" or "Cloud Project" features.
- **Biometric Tone**: We don't track who you are, only how you play (anonymized playing dynamics) to improve our AI's responsiveness.

## 🔒 Security
- All cloud sync data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
- Your neural profiles are isolated and private to your account.

## 🤝 Third Parties
- We do not sell your data to amp manufacturers. We compete with them.
