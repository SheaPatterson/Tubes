# The Sound Chain: 11 Steps to Next-Gen Realism

Our sound computation is divided into 11 distinct stages, combining classic algorithmic modeling with a cutting-edge AI neural layer.

## 🎞️ Stage 1-10: Classic Engine (Offline Capable)

1. **Tone Stack Mapping**: Accurate algorithmic mapping of each amplifier's individual tone stack variable settings.
2. **Architecture Definition**: Unique internal values defining the specific "architecture" of each manufacturer.
3. **Pedal Circuitry**: Translation of transistors, op-amps, and physical circuits into database-quantifiable information.
4. **Preamp Simulation**: Stage-by-stage gain simulation based on the number of 12AX7 tubes.
5. **Power Amp Dynamics**: characteristic sensitivity of heavy-driven tubes (KT88, 6L6, EL34, etc.) including Sag, Bias, and Voltage calculations.
6. **Cabinet Impulse Response (IR)**: Brand-specific data for speaker type and cab size combinations.
7. **Speaker Dynamics**: Simulating "air" movement, depth, and dimension based on frequency output spectrums.
8. **Microphone Positioning**: 3D positioning (X, Y, Z) relative to the cone (Center for bright, edge for warm/flat).
9. **Microphone Character**: Modeling specific profiles for SM57, Condenser, Ribbon, etc., using manufacturer frequency data.
10. **Distance & Room**: Computing the final non-AI variable for distance from the cone.

## 🧠 Stage 11: The AI Neural Layer (Subscription Tier)

11. **Neural Network Status**: Using advanced AI models to analyze the entire signal chain and apply a final layer of realism that recreates unique tonal properties impossible to achieve with traditional algorithms.
