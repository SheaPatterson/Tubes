# The Reasoning: Beyond the Horizon

Why build another amp sim? Because the industry has plateaued.

## 📉 The Industry Plateau
Current modelers focus on **Snapshots**. They take a picture of a tone and try to morph between pictures. It feels static. It doesn't breathe.

## 📈 The Neural Shift
Our reasoning is that **Intelligence** should be the substrate of sound. By modeling the circuit behavior (Classic) AND then applying a corrective Neural Layer (Next Gen), we solve the "uncanny valley" of digital audio.

## 🎯 Our Focus
1. **The Tube Life**: Simulating the actual decay and resurgence of electrons in a vacuum.
2. **The Air**: Not just an IR, but a physics simulation of sound waves hitting a microphone diaphragm.
3. **The Intelligence**: AI that knows what a "Dumble Overdrive Special" sounds like inside a medium-sized wooden room with two SM57s.
