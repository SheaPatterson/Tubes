# Global Accessibility & Economic Empowerment

Our project is rooted in Shea Patterson's vision of overcoming geographic and economic barriers.

## 🌎 Decentralized Access
- **No Borders**: By providing a high-fidelity web engine, we eliminate the need for expensive physical hardware shipments to rural or impoverished areas.
- **Low-Bandwidth Optimization**: Preset data and "Classic" engine weights are optimized for slow internet connections.

## 💼 Skills Acquisition
- Our "How It Works" and "About" pages are designed to teach users the physics of tone, turning our app into an educational platform for future audio engineers.

## 🤝 Community Outreach
- A percentage of "Next Gen" subscriptions will be allocated to providing "Classic" tier access to music schools in underprivileged areas globally.
