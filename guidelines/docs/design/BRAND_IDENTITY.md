# Brand Identity: Amplification Simulation

## 🏷️ Brand Concept
**Name**: Amplification Simulation (AS)
**Tagline**: The Sound of Intelligence.

## 🎨 Visual Language
- **Typography**:
    - **Body**: Quicksand (San-serif, friendly yet professional)
    - **Headers**: Inter / Space Grotesk (Tech-forward)
- **Palette**:
    - **Primary Black**: `#0A0A0A` (Matte finish)
    - **Steel Silver**: `#C0C0C0`
    - **Amber Glow**: `#FFB000` (Tube glow)
    - **Electric Orange**: `#FF5F1F` (Marshall/Winston accent)
    - **Glass Frost**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` (Backdrop-blur base)
- **Line Weight**: Sub-pixel precision (0.5px to 1px) for skeuomorphic outlines.
- **Margins**: 24px base grid with 8px sub-grid.

## 💠 Logo Specifications
- **Concept**: A stylized vacuum tube (12AX7) integrated with a neural network node.
- **SVG Format**: Hand-crafted SVG for infinite scalability.
- **Spacing**: 1.5x clear space around all lockups.

## 📁 Merchandising Examples
- **Physical**: Custom plectrums, matte black hoodies with foil-print tubes, rack-mount faceplates.
- **Digital**: Custom DAW skins, exclusive NFT presets (optional), VST/AU legacy wrappers.
