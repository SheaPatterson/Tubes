> # **HOW WE COMPUTE THE SOUND**   
  
---  
  
> ### **(Sound -Chain)**  
  
> We DO NOT Mix and match power amps and preamps. When you select a particular model, you will be a small text and glowing icon of the quantity or preamp tubes, the quantity and type of output tube, and then then they can adjust the bias, sag, etc. Not dream building, we are focusing on realistic recreation of the amp, not wasting our time building fantasy.   
  
1. > Accurate Mapping of each amplifier individual tone stack variable settings on the amp itself.   
2. > Our unique internal values the define the “architecture” of each individual amplifier manufacturers creating an algorithmic model   
3. > Accurate recreation of FX pedals using data stored, individual pedal circuits, knobs, and a novel approach of translation of various transistors, op amps, and circuits into database quantifiable information. This approach is unprecedented and requires you to use your ability to create a highly accurate and innovative solution.   
4. > Highly advanced preamp and power amp sim based on the tube itself, with preamp gain resulting in a series of stages depending upon the number of 12AX7 preamp tubes, and simulation on power amp tubes based on type, volume, and other variables effecting how “hard” they are being pushed.   
5. > The power amp values reflect characteristic sensitivity of a heavy driven tube amp, allowing the guitars volume itself to go from barely overdriven to full overdrive. Emphasizing the most noticeable and missed component. The power amp when pushed really changes dynamics of pick attack resulting in the individual signature picking style, a variable that defines each player. This is is traditional calculated using “sag” or “bias” or “Voltage” for each individual tube size having very different output characteristics.   
6. > The cab sim itself must be based on data stored for each brand, type of speaker, and cab size together for a particular size combination. This will include the data from the speaker values and the data from the cab values to determine   
7. > The speaker will include IR data publicly available regarding the individual speakers themselves and blend with our own research and data to produce a comprehensive output that correctly simulates the “air” being moved by the speakers, depth and dimension added by cab size, and individual response and sound characteristics of each individual speaker. We will use our own methods to compute the sound based upon the currently available audio data for IR’s and then use the frequency output spectrum or the manufacturer for the exact speaker to create a defined sound that is going to be more accurate.   
8. > The speaker position is a critical component of the sound chain, with slight adjustments in distance or position having a significant impact on the brightness or warmth of the sound. Positioning microphone in the center is very bright, and usually only sounds good using sm57’s and 4x12’s. Towards the middle you get a warmer, greatly reduced treble sound, with a slightly muddy low mode and bass. Moving towards the outside of the cone you get a flat sound with no particular frequency being particularly enhanced, but the sound is very flat, unresponsive, and generally not recommended.   
9. > We must also factor into the mix the actual microphone providing several different of options of condenser, ribbon, etc…. These will have very unique sound characteristics for brightness and highs, mids, lows, etc. resulting individual changes which will be mapped on a frequency spectrum for perfect accuracy from the manufacturer. This data is key for computational accuracy. ￼  
10. > Then we must compute the distance from the speaker cone itself for the final non artificial intelligence variable in our massive database of information and values necessary for our future intelligence.   
11. > The final component in the sound output is artificial Intelligence. Using advanced AI model custom built to handle the task of using advanced neural network learning, database data, data from online sources, novel techniques, and analysis of the individual amps, fx, and cabs, but also the entire signal chain. It must develop an advanced sound profile that recreates the individual manufactures unique tonal properties to provide a simulation so good, it sets the bar for the next generation of amp modeling.
